Garrick Voss — The Iron Wolf
Age: 29 Race: Human Class: Fighter — Level 8 Alignment: Lawful Good Role: Commander & Protector
Garrick is a soldier at heart. Unlike Lucian, he doesn't manipulate his followers. He earns their respect by standing beside them when things become dangerous.
He's calm, disciplined, and extremely difficult to break.
•     Personality: Stoic, honorable, protective, intimidating.
•     Strength: Leadership under pressure.
•     Weakness: His loyalty can become obsessive.
•     Leadership style: Leads by example and expects his followers to hold themselves to his standards.
•     D&D abilities: Battle Master with maneuvers such as Rally, Commander's Strike, and Precision Attack.
•     Secret: Garrick would sacrifice almost anything to protect the society—including his own morality.
Why he's a great leader: His followers don't obey because they're afraid of him. They obey because they would follow him into hell.
Growing up:
Garrick Voss — The Iron Wolf
Garrick grew up in a small frontier settlement where danger was an ordinary part of life.
His father was a former soldier who had settled down to become a blacksmith. His mother ran a small inn that served travelers, hunters, and soldiers passing through.
Garrick grew up surrounded by weapons, armor, and stories of war.
His father taught him to fight at a young age, but he always emphasized one lesson:
"A weapon protects people. It doesn't make you better than them."
Garrick took those words seriously.
As a child, he was quiet and unusually protective of younger children. If someone weaker was being bullied, Garrick would step between them without hesitation.
He wasn't naturally aggressive.
He simply refused to tolerate cruelty.
When Garrick was thirteen, the settlement was attacked by bandits. His father joined the defense while Garrick and his mother helped evacuate civilians.
His father survived—but several people Garrick knew did not.
The event changed him.
For the first time, Garrick understood that courage alone wasn't enough.
People needed discipline, preparation, and someone willing to take responsibility.
At sixteen, Garrick joined a local militia. He quickly distinguished himself, not because he was the strongest fighter, but because he was exceptionally good at keeping people calm.
During one particularly dangerous battle, the commanding officer was killed.
Garrick, only seventeen, took command of a group of terrified soldiers.
He gave them simple orders.
He stayed at the front.
And he refused to retreat until everyone under his command was safely out.
That night, the survivors began calling him the Iron Wolf.
By his early twenties, Garrick had become a respected commander.
But he eventually became disillusioned with traditional military leadership. He saw nobles sending soldiers to die for political games and officers protecting their careers instead of their people.
He began searching for something different.
Something where loyalty actually meant something.
That's when he eventually encountered the secret society.
And for the first time, Garrick believed he'd found people worth fighting for.
