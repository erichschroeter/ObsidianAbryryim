### Questions to ask DQ candidates 
Ask your candidate these three hypothetical dilemmas to expose their true priorities, reveal how they handle hidden failure, and test their limits under pressure:

#### 1. The Betrayal Dilemma

> **"A trusted inner-circle member commits a high-stakes betrayal for what they believed was a noble reason, risking exposure of the entire society. How do you handle them?"**

- **What it tests:** Absolute ethics vs. pragmatic cruelty vs. emotional attachment.
- **What to look for:**
    - **Garrick:** Look for whether he enforces strict justice, attempts to shield them from consequences out of protective loyalty, or crosses a moral line to cover it up.
    - **Lucian:** Look for whether he re-engineers the narrative to turn the betrayal into an asset, or if his hidden care for the member causes him to hesitate.
    - **Elias:** Look for whether he treats the member as a mere variable in a larger prophecy, or quietly eliminates/silences them without consulting anyone else.

#### 2. The Transmutation of Truth

> **"To save fifty innocent lives and advance the society's ultimate mission, you must publicly frame an innocent person for a major crime. Do you do it?"**

- **What it tests:** Moral rigidity vs. utilitarian ethics ("the ends justify the means").
- **What to look for:**
    - **Garrick:** If he says yes without deep conflict, his secret desire to "sacrifice his own morality" is already consuming him. If he says no, he proves his core ethics are unshakeable.
    - **Lucian:** He will likely say yes effortlessly—the real test is whether he tells _you_ the truth about how comfortable he is doing it.
    - **Elias:** Look for whether he views the innocent person's fate as pre-ordained or unavoidable based on his visions.

#### 3. The Fallibility Test

> **"You discover that a major strategic decision you made resulted in catastrophic harm to our mission. No one else knows it was your fault. What is your immediate next move?"**

- **What it tests:** Accountable leadership vs. cover-ups and manipulation.
- **What to look for:**
    - **Garrick:** Expect immediate self-accountability and taking full responsibility in front of his followers, even at personal cost.
    - **Lucian:** Watch if his instinct is to manipulate the narrative so the blame falls elsewhere or vanishes entirely.
    - **Elias:** Watch if he uses the shield of "fate" or "a necessary step toward a greater vision" to hide his operational failure.
