The Journal of Vaelithra, Keeper of the Verdant Eclipse
Personal Diary Logs — Restricted Entry
Log 001 — The First Egg

The first egg has been placed beneath the roots. The others laughed when I said life could be created without blessing, without birth, without the touch of another soul. They called my studies forbidden. They called me a fool. Now I hold creation itself in my hands. The shell pulses with magic. The creature inside listens before it ever breathes. It knows my voice. It knows its purpose. The army begins.

Log 002 — The Master’s First Command

The Master contacted me today. I heard his  voice. The first command was simple. Create something that cannot be defeated. No more instructions. No more questions. That is why the Master chose me. Others need to be guided. I only need a vision. The living world above believes it is safe. They have no idea what grows beneath their feet.

Log 003 — The Hatchery

The underground chambers are complete. The roots of the ancient waters now cradle thousands of eggs, They call this corruption. I call it evolution. The druids wanted me to protect life. The wizards wanted me to study it. Neither understood the truth. Life is a weapon. And I have learned how to forge it.

Log 004 — Observations

The first creatures have begun to move. They do not cry. They do not fear. They do not hesitate. They are everything the living are not. The living are ruled by emotion...Fear...Doubt...Mercy. My creations know only purpose. They will not abandon me. 

Log 005 — The Living Will Pay

I have not forgotten. The kingdoms above celebrate their victories while pretending they have no blood on their hands. They burned. They conquered. They destroyed. Then they wrote songs about their greatness. Soon they will sing a different song. The song of my army. The song of their downfall.

Log 006 — Growth

The eggs are changing faster than expected. Each new hatchling is stronger than the last. The magic is becoming something greater. Something beyond wizardry. Something beyond druidic power. The old ways are dying. A new nature is being born. And I am its mother.

Log 007 — The Forest Rejects Me

The trees whisper my name like a curse. The spirits refuse to answer. Animals flee from the my tunnels. They fear what I create. They do not understand. Nature has always survived through change. The weak disappear. The strong remain. I am simply helping nature remember.

Log 008 — The Master Returns

The Master came again. Only twice. Two visits. Two commands. That is all I have needed. The Master looked upon the army and said only: "Soon." A single word. A promise. A warning. The final stage begins.

Log 009 — The Names Written in Stone

I have carved the names of my enemies into the walls. Kings....Generals....Priests....Heroes. All who thought themselves untouchable. They will see what happens when the forgotten stop begging. They will learn what happens when the dead and the living no longer matter. Only victory matters.
Log 010 — The Army Sleeps

Thousands of eggs. Thousands of heartbeats. A thousand soldiers waiting beneath the earth. Above, the world continues. Children laugh. Merchants trade. Armies march. They believe tomorrow belongs to them. They are wrong. Tomorrow belongs to what I have created.

Log 011 — The First March

The first generation stands ready. They move when I command. They stop when I command. They fight when I command. No fear. No hesitation. No weakness. The perfect army.

Log 012 — Doubt

For the first time, I wonder...Have I created servants? Or have I created something greater than myself? The creatures look at me. They recognize me. But sometimes... I feel as though they are waiting. Watching. Learning.

Log 013 — The Final Preparations

The chambers are empty. The eggs are gone. The army is complete. Soon they will rise from beneath the roots and spread across the lands. The living will finally understand. Mercy was their gift. Fear will be mine.

Log 014 — Tomorrow

Tomorrow begins the new age. The kingdoms will fall. The old powers will crumble. The world will remember the name Vaelithra. Not as a villain. Not as a monster. But as the one who gave nature its final form.

Log 015 — Final Entry

The army waits. The Master waits. The world waits. They do not know it yet, but their time is ending. They thought monsters came from darkness. They were wrong. The greatest monsters are created by those who have been ignored for too long. The living had their chance.

Now...

they will pay...
