# Source: Telemanus ti Fjall Character Sheet

This document records the official character parameters and personal details of [[Telemanus ti Fjall]], an Orc Ranger.

## Key Takeaways
- **Ranger Training**: Level 4 Ranger with a Guide background. Proficient in Cartographer's Tools, Survival, Stealth, Nature, and Athletics.
- **Combat Style**: Uses dual-weapon fighting with a scimitar and a shortsword.
- **Personality**: Solitary, stoic, survivalist, naturalist, combatant. Selfish, hypocrite, and unapproachable, putting self before others.
- **Connections**: Deep memories of the [[Zephyr Mountains]] and nature; carries a simple gift from his grandmother.
