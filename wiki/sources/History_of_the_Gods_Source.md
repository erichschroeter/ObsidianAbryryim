# Source: History of the Gods

This document details the nature of godhood and timeline in Abryryim.

## Key Takeaways
- **Purpose**: Gods exist to serve the world.
- **Mortality**: During the transition year between Ages, gods walk the world in flesh and can be killed.
- **The Sanctification**: The transition year during which the ten prophecies are fulfilled.
- **Deity Limit**: Exactly ten individuals (old or new gods) ascend to divinity at the start of a new Age.
