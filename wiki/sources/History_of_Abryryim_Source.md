# Source: History of Abryryim

This document summarizes the historical cycle of Abryryim as described in World Anvil.

## Key Takeaways
- **The Cycle**: The world follows a 1,000-year cycle of ascension. Every 1,000 years, the current deities must return to mortality to allow a new Age's prophecies to be fulfilled.
- **Ascension**: Individuals who fulfill these prophecies ascend to godhood for the next Age.
- **The Ages**: Dawn, Magic, Steel, Nature, Lava, Waxing, Waning, Light, Darkness, and Dusk.
- **Landmasses**: Yenos, Kreittens, and Quatois, each featuring different cultures and resources.
