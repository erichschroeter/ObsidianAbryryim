# Source: Owen Blackwood Backstory

This document outlines the early life, military service, and moral journey of [[Owen Blackwood]], a Human Fighter with a Soldier background.

## Key Takeaways
- **Origin**: Born in a small timber settlement far from [[Pistivo]]. Enlisted in a local lord's military for coin and survival.
- **Military Trauma**: Disobeyed orders during an aggressive raid on a helpless settlement to make an example of it. Saved several families but took a beating for insubordination. Left the service with severe distrust of ranks and authority.
- **Mentor**: Met Devlin Quicksilver on the road, an older adventurer who taught him responsibility, patience, and how "surviving is not the same as living."
- **Present Day**: Traveled to [[Pistivo]] out of respect for Devlin's funeral, finding himself tied to memory and unfinished business left behind.
