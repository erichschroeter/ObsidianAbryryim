# Source: Brokkur Stonehelm Backstory

This document outlines the childhood, personal tragedy, and inventions of [[Brokkur Stonehelm]], a Dwarf Artificer of the Armorer subclass.

## Key Takeaways
- **Homeland**: [[Windford]], the City of Vaults.
- **Tragedy**: At age 12, his father Thrain was killed when a coastal cliff vault routine inspection flooded during a seismic tremor. Lord Grizfane's security protocols sealed and flooded the vault automatically to protect treasure. Brokkur was too weak to lift the stone block pinning his father, or to throw the manual override lock.
- **Inventions**: Dedicated his talent to preservation over war. Created the Aegis Dome (portable force field), Stun Grenades (non-violent crowd control), and Biotic Bullets (alchemical injector darts fired from a rifle to heal from afar).
- **Mother**: Astrid Stonehelm, Veteran Grid-Overseer of Windford Mariculture Cooperative. Her work in heavy dwarven diving suits (brass and mithral exoskeletons) inspired Brokkur's Armorer subclass and his search for armor protection as "shelter."
- **Friends**: Vara Ironlung (clam farm diver, Brokkur's secret crush) and Kildrak the Scribe (arcane conduit who smuggled magical blueprints for Brokkur).
- **Goal**: Return to Windford, bypass Lord Grizfane's unbreachable vault defenses, and retrieve Thrain's remains. Needs [[Dark Diamond Ore]] and the [[Forge of Heaven]] to construct an ultra-pressure protective suit.
