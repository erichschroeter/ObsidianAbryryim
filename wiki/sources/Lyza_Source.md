# Source: Lyza Backstory

This document details the background and magic studies of [[Lyza]], a Human Wizard with a Sage background.

## Key Takeaways
- **Profession**: Worked as a scribe for most of her life, copying books, letters, and scrolls.
- **Education**: Learned magic by copying and practicing spells from the magical texts she copied daily. Mother is Lady Aurelix Sunspire, a magic teacher at a magical college where Lyza worked.
- **Connections**: Extensive acquaintances among scholars, librarians, and travelers. Came to [[Pistivo]] for the funeral of [[Owen Blackwood]]'s mentor, Devlin Quicksilver.
