# Source: Baronies of Abryryim

This document details three prominent baronies: Silverbrook, Oakhollow, and Stonefell.

## Key Takeaways
- **Barony of Silverbrook**: Crossroad rivers and roads guard, ruled by [[Lord Edrin Silverbrook]]. Centers on trade and diplomacy.
- **Barony of Oakhollow**: Ancient woodlands realm, ruled by [[Lady Mara Oakheart]]. Focuses on independence and natural traditions.
- **Barony of Stonefell**: Mountain pass guard, ruled by [[Lord Garric Stonefist]]. Focuses on watchfulness and defense against northern threats.
