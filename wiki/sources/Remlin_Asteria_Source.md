# Source: Remlin Ast'eria Backstory

This document outlines the divine alignment, Druidic training, and celestial visions of [[Remlin Ast'eria]], a High Elf Druid of the Circle of the Stars.

## Key Takeaways
- **Heritage**: Father died at birth. Mother was a devout high elf priestess of Oros al'Calhoon (the [[Twin Soul]]), who departed for the Feywild, leaving young Remlin to be raised by Druid elders.
- **Training**: Honed cosmology and celestial studies under the *Turning Sky* Circle of Star Druids and Orcish Shaman allies in the mountains.
- **The Vision**: Visited by a vision of Oros al'Calhoon, revealing a possible prophecy of the end times: *"Comfort…heavenly might guiding all…light."*
- **Owlbear Hide Star Map**: Studied in the Feywild and cured an Owlbear hide, etching it with astrological visions (The Chalice Bearer, The Archer Unbound, The Star-Dragon, The Broken Crown, The Twin Paths, and the Extinguished Sky).
- **Goal**: Walks among mortals to seek the rest of the Prophecy, test stars' guidance, and discover if fate can be changed.
