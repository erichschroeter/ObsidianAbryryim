# Source: Knights of Prophecy

Details the divine tracker and prophetic guardian subclass/organization.

## Key Takeaways
- Knights receive a sliver of divine insight into one ascension prophecy of the next Cycle.
- They submit details to the Council at the [[Island of Prophecy]] and protect the prophecy from subversion.
- Focuses on divination, truth enforcement, survival tracking, and roll-influencing Vision Dice.
