# Source: Nature's Savant

Details the nature-bonded Wizard subclass.

## Key Takeaways
- Conjuration spells copy time/cost halved.
- Allows summoning a primal beast companion (*Gaia's Ally*) whose stats scale with Wizard level.
- Provides spell sharing, multiattack, and defensive synergy at higher levels.
