# Source: Araminta Rosk Backstory

This document outlines the background and abilities of [[Araminta Rosk]] (also known as "Min"), a Half-Elf Cleric of the War Domain.

## Key Takeaways
- **Origin**: Raised in a mountain town near [[Ebonhaven]] as a blacksmith's daughter.
- **Profession**: Apprenticed as a master glasswright under Garris Remley from [[Da'Hal Kempa]].
- **Divine Calling**: Trapped in a temple during an attack, she defended worshippers and children alone. She received a blessing of Oros al'Calhoon (the [[Twin Soul]]), granting her divine powers.
- **Aesthetics & Weapons**: Carries a hammer and a glaive, using her glassworking background to find beauty in history and art.
