# Source: Eudora Aetherwyn Backstory

This document outlines the family lineage, academic chaos, and relic reclamation quest of [[Eudora Aetherwyn]], a Human Wild Magic Sorcerer.

## Key Takeaways
- **Family**: House Aetherwyn, a highly respected magical family in [[Windmire]], aiming to join the Council of Tides. Every third generation, a sorcerer of exceptional power is born to them.
- **Uncontrolled Magic**: Born with wild, volatile power that private tutors could not help her control. Sent to the Academy of Arcane Studies, where she became a campus legend for accidental magical chaos and graduated despite academic uncertainty.
- **Distinguishing Trait**: A wild magic surge permanently transformed her hair into a shifting display of vibrant colors.
- **Relic Storefront Job**: Sent by her family to [[Ebonhaven]] to work at a relic reclamation storefront to keep her out of trouble.
- **Quest**: Unstable artifacts and relics are drawn to her. Set out on an expedition to reclaim the Rod of Saint Fe'tuth, a sacred bone relic.
