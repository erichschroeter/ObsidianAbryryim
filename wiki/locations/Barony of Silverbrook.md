# Barony of Silverbrook

The Barony of Silverbrook guards the crossroads of rivers and roads linking the north and south.

## Description
Granted to [[Lord Edrin Silverbrook]] for his wisdom in negotiation and uniting rival settlements, the Barony of Silverbrook is a crucial center of trade and diplomacy.

## Heraldry
- **Symbol**: A bridge (signifying connection and safe passage) and a star (representing guidance and hope).
