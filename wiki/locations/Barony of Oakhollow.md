# Barony of Oakhollow

The Barony of Oakhollow lies within ancient, sheltering woodlands.

## Description
Founded by [[Lady Mara Oakheart]], a protector of the natural world and the old ways, the Barony of Oakhollow is characterized by steadfast independence and deep respect for tradition.

## Heraldry
- **Symbol**: An oak (signifying strength and endurance) and acorns (symbolizing growth and renewal).
