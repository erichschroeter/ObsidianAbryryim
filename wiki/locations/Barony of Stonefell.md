# Barony of Stonefell

The Barony of Stonefell holds the mountain passes that defend the realm from northern threats.

## Description
Granted to [[Lord Garric Stonefist]], a courageous warrior, the Barony of Stonefell maintains an unbreakable watch over the mountain passes.

## Heraldry
- **Symbol**: Mountains (representing endurance and defense) and a tower (standing for vigilance).
