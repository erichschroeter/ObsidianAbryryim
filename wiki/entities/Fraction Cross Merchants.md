# Fraction Cross Merchants

The Fraction Cross Merchants is a daring merchant guild operating across hazardous regions.

## Operations
- **Lava Trade**: They are one of the few guilds willing to travel across the dangerous lava and sulfur fields.
- **Continental Transit**: They manage the transport of goods between continents by land or sea.
- **Security**: Because of the high danger, the guards protecting their underground caverns are exceptionally well-paid.
