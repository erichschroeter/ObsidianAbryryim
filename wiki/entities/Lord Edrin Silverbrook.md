# Lord Edrin Silverbrook

Lord Edrin Silverbrook is the ruler and founder of the [[Barony of Silverbrook]].

## Biography
Lord Edrin was granted the barony in recognition of his wisdom in negotiation and his skill in uniting rival settlements. Under his leadership, Silverbrook became a renowned center of trade and diplomacy.
