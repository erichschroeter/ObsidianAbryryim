# Lady Mara Oakheart

Lady Mara Oakheart is the founder and ruler of the [[Barony of Oakhollow]].

## Biography
Lady Mara is a staunch protector of the old ways and the natural world. She established the Barony of Oakhollow in the ancient woodlands, instilling a deep respect for forest traditions and independence.
