# Liars Syndicate

The Liars Syndicate is the largest secret organization in the world, operating as an elite guild of hired killers.

## Code & Philosophy
- **Contract Killers**: They kill purely for money, regardless of the target's alignment (good or evil).
- **The Code**: They strictly forbid killing children under the age of 12.
- **Orphan Protection**: Children orphaned under 12 are placed under their protection and trained to become the next generation of the Syndicate.
- **Conflicts**: Historically defeated and dispersed the [[Jinog-Tis]] group during the 3rd Age Cleansing.
