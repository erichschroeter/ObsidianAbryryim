# Owen Blackwood

Owen Blackwood is a Human Fighter and former soldier who travels the roads of Abryryim as an independent sword-for-hire.

## Background
Owen was born in a small timber village. To escape hard years, he enlisted in the army, where he became a skilled shield guard. However, his service ended when he disobeyed orders during a brutal raid on a peaceful village, choosing to rescue families instead of burning them. After receiving a punishment for insubordination, he left the service.

He later met Devlin Quicksilver, an experienced traveler who mentored him on road survival and craftsmanship, steering him away from bitterness. Owen recently arrived in [[Pistivo]] to attend Devlin's funeral.
