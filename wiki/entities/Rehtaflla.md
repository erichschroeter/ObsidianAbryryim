# Rehtaflla

The Rehtaflla is one of the most powerful and influential secret societies in Abryryim.

## Lore
- **Origin**: Emerged early in the Second Age as a secret esoteric order.
- **Teachings**: Instructs members in deep wisdom regarding nature, the physical world, and the spiritual world.
- **Occult Magic**: They utilize occult magic aimed at engendering both personal spiritual transformation and global transformation.
