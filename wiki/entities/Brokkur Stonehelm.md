# Brokkur Stonehelm

Brokkur Stonehelm is a Dwarf Artificer of the Armorer subclass, hailing from the coastal dwarven city of [[Windford]].

## Background
Brokkur grew up in [[Windford]], showing an early genius for mechanics over traditional stonecraft. His father Thrain, a surveyor, died in a flooded coastal vault when Brokkur was 12 because the automated security protocol of Lord Grizfane Goldentooth sealed and flooded the sector.

His mother, Astrid Stonehelm, works in heavy deep-sea diving exoskeletons. Seeing her disappear into these brass and mithral protective shells gave Brokkur the inspiration to build armor as "shelter" to protect his companions from loss.

## Inventions
- **Aegis Dome**: Hexagonal portable kinetic barrier.
- **Stun Grenade**: Flashbangs to disable threats without causing structural cave-ins.
- **Biotic Bullets**: Pressurized alchemical injector darts to heal allies at a distance.

## Current Quest
Brokkur is searching for [[Dark Diamond Ore]] and the secrets of the [[Forge of Heaven]] to construct an advanced protective suit capable of withstanding the immense pressure of the flooded vaults in Windford, so he can retrieve his father's body.
