# Lyza

Lyza is a Human Wizard who gained her magical expertise through a life spent copying arcane texts.

## Background
Lyza worked as a professional scribe for many years, copying books, letters, and magical scrolls in various scriptoria. She grew up in a magical college where her mother, Lady Aurelix Sunspire, was a magical instructor. This environment allowed Lyza to study wizardry and copy spells into her own spellbook during her spare time.

She traveled to [[Pistivo]] for the funeral of Devlin Quicksilver, where she joined a group of adventurers.

## Characteristics
- **Race**: Human
- **Class**: Wizard
- **Background**: Scribe / Sage
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
