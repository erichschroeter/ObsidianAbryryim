# Remlin Ast'eria

Remlin Ast'eria (also known as "Owl") is a High Elf Druid of the Circle of the Stars.

## Background
Remlin was born on a night of rare stellar alignment. He was raised by Druid elders after his mother, a priestess of Oros al'Calhoon, left for the Feywild. He trained in the peaks under the Turning Sky Circle of Star Druids and Orcish Shamans, learning astronomy and planar matrices.

During a 50-year stay in the Feywild with his mother, he crafted an Owlbear hide cloak, etching the inside with fragments of a celestial prophecy map. He has returned to Abryryim to pursue his visions and understand if destiny can be guided or mitigated.

## Prophecy Fragment
Remlin is guided by a fragment of vision: **"Comfort…heavenly might guiding all…light."**

## Characteristics
- **Race**: High Elf
- **Class**: Druid (Circle of the Stars)
- **Age**: ~200 years
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Aesthetic**: Owlbear hide constellation cloak
