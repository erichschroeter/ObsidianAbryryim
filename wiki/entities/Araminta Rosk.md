# Araminta Rosk

Araminta "Min" Rosk is a Half-Elf War Domain Cleric devoted to Oros al'Calhoon.

## Background
Min grew up near [[Ebonhaven]], the daughter of a local blacksmith. As a teenager, she became an apprentice to Garris Remley, a traveling master glasswright from [[Da'Hal Kempa]]. Under his tutelage, she traveled across Abryryim restoring stained-glass windows in temples, cathedrals, and chapels, capturing history and religious lore through her art.

## Divine Elevation
During a crisis where creatures breached a temple of Oros al'Calhoon, Min stood alone to protect trapped children and clergy while her mentor was separated from her. For her immense courage and selflessness, Oros al'Calhoon, the Goddess of Change and Guidance, bestowed her with divine grace, granting her the powers of a War Domain Cleric.

## Characteristics
- **Race**: Half-Elf
- **Class**: Cleric (War Domain)
- **Age**: 25
- **Weapons**: Hammer and Glaive
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
