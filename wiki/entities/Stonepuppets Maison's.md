# Stonepuppets Maison's

Stonepuppets Maison's is an ancient, secret guild of builders operating both physically and metaphorically underground.

## Operations
- **Temple Builders**: They are the exclusive builders of the temples of the gods, a role they have held since the First Age.
- **Secrets**: They protect the true plans and secrets of every temple they build, possessing an unending knowledge of sacred architecture.
