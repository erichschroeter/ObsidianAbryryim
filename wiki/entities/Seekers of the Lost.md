# Seekers of the Lost

The Seekers of the Lost is an organization dedicated to exploration and reclaiming lost history.

## Services
- **Goal**: Discover lost temples, cities, and artifacts forgotten in time.
- **Roles**: They operate as guides and teachers within cities, offering their services to adventuring groups.
