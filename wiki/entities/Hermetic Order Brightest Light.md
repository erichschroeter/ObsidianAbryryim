# Hermetic Order Brightest Light

The Hermetic Order Brightest Light is a quiet, mystical order that only engages with the world during [[The Cleansing]].

## Role in the Cycle
- **Proclamation**: One year before the Cleansing, members emerge to proclaim the coming of the new Age.
- **Stabilization**: They help stabilize cities and the land as the world transitions through the upheavals of the Cleansing.
