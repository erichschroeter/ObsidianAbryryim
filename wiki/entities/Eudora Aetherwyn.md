# Eudora Aetherwyn

Eudora Aetherwyn is a Human Wild Magic Sorcerer hailing from the city of [[Windmire]].

## Background
Eudora is a member of House Aetherwyn, a prominent magical family in [[Windmire]]. Historically, every third generation of her family produces a sorcerer of exceptional power. Eudora was born with this lineage, but her magic proved highly chaotic and difficult to control, leading to her being sent to the Academy of Arcane Studies. At the Academy, she became famous for accidental magical mishaps and a wild surge that permanently turned her hair shifting, vibrant colors.

## Relic Reclamation
Her family sent her to [[Ebonhaven]] to work safely in a storefront cataloging relics, hoping to minimize trouble. However, her presence naturally draws unstable magical artifacts. She is currently on an expedition to find the Rod of Saint Fe'tuth.

## Characteristics
- **Race**: Human
- **Class**: Sorcerer (Wild Magic)
- **Homeland**: [[Windmire]]
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Good / Neutral
- **Focus**: Softly glowing crystal pendant
