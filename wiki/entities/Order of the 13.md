# Order of the 13

The Order of the 13 is a hidden cult dedicated to powerful devils.

## Operations
- **Dark Rituals**: They perform secret rituals to enhance their power, acquire wealth, and fulfill dark desires.
- **Apocalyptic Goals**: They operate in secret within major cities, actively working to bring about the end of the world.
