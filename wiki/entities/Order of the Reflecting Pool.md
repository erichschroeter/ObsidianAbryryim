# Order of the Reflecting Pool

The Order of the Reflecting Pool is a secret organization of seekers focused on preventing mass death.

## Philosophy & Operations
- **Mission**: Hunt for god artifacts and other powerful items that could cause catastrophe or mass death.
- **Agnosticism**: They do not serve any specific god; instead, they serve all the gods in the interest of preventing history-altering disasters.
