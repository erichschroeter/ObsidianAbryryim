# Jinog-Tis

The Jinog-Tis is a legendary faction of dual-identity killers.

## History & Tactics
- **Dual Identity**: They are regular farmers by day, but operate as fearless killers by night.
- **Fearlessness**: Famous for their willingness to cut down targets regardless of heavy security. They have successfully executed viziers, sultans, and church leaders.
- **Fall & Dispersion**: Over three Ages, they were ruled by eight Grand Masters. During the 3rd Age Cleansing, they were essentially destroyed by the [[Liars Syndicate]] and forced to disperse.
- **Vendetta**: The surviving Jinog-Tis actively seek revenge against the Syndicate.
