# Telemanus ti Fjall

Telemanus ti Fjall is an Orc Ranger who serves as a skilled wilderness guide in Abryryim.

## Background
Telemanus is a level 4 Ranger from the [[Zephyr Mountains]]. He lives a stoic and solitary life, surviving off the land. While he has a good-natured side deep down, his outer demeanor is highly selfish, unapproachable, and cautious, prioritizing his own security and survival. He carries a small, sentimental gift from his grandmother.

In combat, he utilizes dual-wielding, fighting with a Scimitar and a Shortsword.

## Characteristics
- **Race**: Orc
- **Class**: Ranger (Level 4)
- **Background**: Guide
- **Homeland**: [[Zephyr Mountains]]
- **Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
