# Lord Garric Stonefist

Lord Garric Stonefist is the founder and ruler of the [[Barony of Stonefell]].

## Biography
Lord Garric is a legendary warrior whose courage never faltered. He was granted the Barony of Stonefell to guard the mountain passes and protect the realm from northern threats.
