# Sect of the Divine Reach

The Sect of the Divine Reach is an organization of religious fanatics who believe they represent the hand of the gods.

## Details
- **Beliefs**: They serve the divine hand of all gods: past, present, and the newly ascended.
- **Zealotry**: They hunt down anyone who opposes what they deem to be the absolute truth of the gods.
