# Horsemen of Phitare

The Horsemen of Phitare is a powerful criminal organization composed of ex-prisoners and criminals.

## Activities
- **Raids**: They raid settlements and trade routes, utilizing the acquired wealth to subvert and control cities.
- **Influence**: They have agents embedded in almost every city, expanding their grip on regional politics and commerce.
