# Barron Calvin Aldric

Barron Calvin Aldric of the Key and Sword is the current ruling noble of the [[Barony of the Key and Sword]] and a leader of [[House Aldric]].

## Background
Calvin Aldric is a direct descendant of the legendary [[Sir Aldric the Warden]]. He maintains the defense, law, and order of the western frontier of Abryryim from his ancestral lands.

During the battle of Brookelille, he commissioned the soldier [[Owen Blackwood]] to hold the left flank, later knighting him and declaring him a hero of the land.

## Characteristics
- **Title**: Barron of the Key and Sword
- **House**: [[House Aldric]]
- **Alignment**: Lawful Neutral / Good
