# Nature's Savant Subclass

A Wizard subclass focused on conjuration and a deep bond with the natural world.

## Subclass Features
- **Conjuration Savant (2nd Level)**: Cost and time to copy Conjuration spells into the spellbook is halved.
- **Gaia's Ally (2nd Level)**: Summon a primal beast companion that scales with Wizard level.
- **Improved Ally (6th Level)**: Command the beast to Dash, Disengage, or Help as a bonus action. Attacks count as magical.
- **Gaia's Fury (10th Level)**: Beast companion makes two attacks. Beast increases one physical ability score by +2.
- **Nature's Bond (15th Level)**: Wizard's self-targeted spells can also affect the beast companion.
