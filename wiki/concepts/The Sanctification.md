# The Sanctification

The Sanctification is the transition year between two Ages in the [[Cycle of Ascension]].

## Details
- **Year of Mortality**: During this single year, the current deities walk the world in mortal form and are vulnerable to being killed.
- **Fulfillment of Prophecies**: The ten prophecies of the Age are fulfilled during this year.
- **The Ten Ascendants**: Exactly ten individuals ascend to godhood at the end of the year. This can include old deities reclaiming their status or new mortals ascending.
