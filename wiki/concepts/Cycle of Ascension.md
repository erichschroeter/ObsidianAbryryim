# Cycle of Ascension

The Cycle of Ascension is the fundamental law of time and divinity in the world of Abryryim.

## Rules of the Cycle
- **1000-Year Divinity**: Deities only possess divinity for exactly 1,000 years (representing one Age) before they are forced to return to mortality.
- **Return to Flesh**: Taking on flesh and blood is required to ensure fulfillment of the new prophecies demanded by each Age.
- **Fulfillment and Ascension**: The mortal beings who fulfill these prophecies ascend to godhood for the subsequent Age, perpetuating the cycle.

## Chronology of Ages
The world progresses through ten Ages in sequence:
1. Dawn
2. Magic
3. Steel
4. Nature
5. Lava
6. Waxing
7. Waning
8. Light
9. Darkness
10. Dusk
