# Knights of Prophecy Subclass

A subclass adopted by individuals touched by a sliver of divine power, allowing them to safeguard the prophecies of ascension.

## Subclass Features
- **Spellcasting Expansion**: Automatically prepares spells like *protection from evil and good*, *Zone of Truth*, *beacon of hope*, *freedom of movement*, and *legend lore*.
- **Divine Tracker**: Double proficiency bonus in Investigation or Survival to track enemies of the faith.
- **Prophetic Vision**: Channel vision dice (4d6, becoming 1d8 at 11th level) to modify rolls, reduce damage, or cure ailments.
- **Truth Sets You Free (7th Level)**: Emit a *Zone of Truth* centered on self with advantage on Insight checks.
- **Purity of Spirit (15th Level)**: Stop aging, immune to artificial aging, and immune to roll advantage/disadvantage modifications from external spells/effects.
